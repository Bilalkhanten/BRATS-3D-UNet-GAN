@appendix[hard-supp] Supplementary Results of the Hard Synthetic Benchmark

This appendix collects the complete tables and figures behind Section @sec:hard. All values are computed from the run directories of the hard benchmark (160 synthetic phantoms; 32 test phantoms of fold 0; 128 development phantoms in three folds) by the same code that produced the chapter; none of them is a result on BraTS. Phantoms are identified by the last three digits of their case number.

For WT and TC the accuracy is highest in the central slices (A5: 0.929 and 0.895) and falls towards the poles of the lesion (0.746 and nan), where partial volume and the blur of the labels dominate. For ET the profile does not follow this pattern: the mean over the central positions (0.519 for A5, 0.432 for A0) is higher than that over the outer positions (nan and nan). A plausible reason, which the data do not test, is the geometry of the hard phantoms: the enhancing tumour is a thin shell, so that a central slice cuts it as a ring whose thickness is a few voxels and a boundary error of one voxel removes a large fraction of it, whereas the slices near the poles cut it tangentially as a compact disc. The ranking of the two networks along the axis changes between positions, in agreement with the small and unstable difference of the means.

The histograms show where it comes from: in the cross-validated panel a large group of phantoms of both grades receives a probability close to zero or close to one irrespective of its true grade, and in the test panel the 24 HGG-like phantoms are spread over a wide range of probabilities while the few LGG-like phantoms lie at its lower end.
